HASP LAB

The Human-centred and AI Security, Ethics and Privacy (HASP) Lab is a research lab working at the intersection between Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, and Human-Computer Interaction. HASP is based at INGENIO, an interdisciplinary research centre and joint venture between the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) and Universitat Politecnica de Valencia.
