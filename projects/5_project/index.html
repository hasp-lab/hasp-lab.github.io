Online Harms

We also study the online harms that users may be exposed to when navigating the cyber space. We particularly focus on online harms in online social networks, including: toxic, stereotyped and biased language; phenomenons like echo chambers and partizan (and cross-partizan) speech; and misinformation/disinformation.

We take an AI-based approach, where we use AI methods as a tool to study online harms. As part of this, we use the methods that we developed to study ethics in AI (see our work on AI Ethics), for instance the methods we developed to measure AI bias. Interestingly, AI very often reproduces existing instances of discrimination in the offline world by either inheriting the biases of prior decision makers, or simply reflecting widespread prejudices in society. Therefore, by developing methods to study AI discrimination, this also enables us to understand instances of human discrimination. For instance, we have applied our methods to discover biases in natural language processing models to discover dangerous prejudices in online communities using their own language (usually containing slang).
